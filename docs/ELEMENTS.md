# Brindle: elements Pineo knows

102 elements. Ask "where is …", "how do I …" or "show me …" about any of them.


## Everywhere (tab bar, header)

| Element | What it is | How to get there |
|---|---|---|
| Notifications bell | Round bell button in the top right corner of every screen, with a badge showing unread alerts | Tap the bell at the top right of any screen |
| Home tab | First button in the bottom navigation bar; opens the Home screen with balance and accounts | Tap Home at the bottom left of the screen |
| Cards tab | Second button in the bottom navigation bar; opens the Cards screen where cards are blocked, limits changed and the PIN shown | Tap Cards in the bottom bar |
| Send tab | Round raised button in the middle of the bottom navigation bar; opens the Send money form | Tap the round Send button in the middle of the bottom bar |
| Activity tab | Fourth button in the bottom navigation bar; opens the full list of payments and transactions | Tap Activity in the bottom bar |
| Profile tab | Last button in the bottom navigation bar; opens Profile with settings, security, notifications and support | Tap Profile at the bottom right of the screen |

| Amount | Large field in the How much section of Send money where the amount to send is typed | Open Send, scroll to How much and type the amount |

| Note for the recipient | Text box under the amount for a message or payment reference the recipient will see | Open Send and write in Note for the recipient |

| Schedule for later | Switch in the When section of Send money that reveals the date and repeat fields | Open Send, scroll to When and turn on Schedule for later |
| Send on date | Date field that appears when Schedule for later is on; the day the money leaves | Open Send, turn on Schedule for later and pick a date in Send on |
| Repeat | Dropdown that appears when Schedule for later is on; repeats the transfer weekly or monthly | Open Send, turn on Schedule for later and choose Repeat |
| Save this recipient | Switch in the When section of Send money that keeps the recipient for next time | Open Send, scroll down and turn on Save this recipient |
| Transfer summary | Box near the bottom of Send money listing what they receive, the transfer fee and the total leaving the account | Open Send and scroll down to Summary |
| Confirm details checkbox | Checkbox above the submit button confirming the recipient and amount were checked; required before sending | Open Send, scroll to the bottom and tick the confirmation box |
| Submit transfer | Large orange button at the very bottom of the Send money form that sends the transfer | Open Send, fill in the form, scroll to the bottom and tap Submit transfer |
